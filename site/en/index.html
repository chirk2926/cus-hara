HOW TO PLAY

One shift, four customers.

The customer's ANGER keeps rising while you think. Apologizing nonstop won't work, and neither will caving in. Stick to the store's rules and send them home satisfied.

MAIN STAGE

The arcade "Game Paradise"

Crane games everywhere. Every "I can't win" is somehow your fault. Don't just talk: check the machine, fix it, and win them over.

  • AI

    Different customers every time

    An AI plays the customer. Absurd ones, chatty ones, people who just want the restroom.

  • 15machines

    Tune the crane games

    Grab and move the prizes, set the claw power and payout rate. Every change shifts complaints and sales.

  • OPEN

    Repair medal games

    Jammed medals, empty hoppers, a lottery wheel that won't spin. Open the cabinet and fix it.

  • ID

    Age checks and cabinet slammers

    Age checks, change machines, customers who slam the cabinet. Handle realistic trouble with store actions.

  • 155titles

    Titles and mental rank

    155 titles to earn and a mental rank from 1 to 20, plus a backdrop gacha and character dress-up.

  • 0money

    No real money

    The gacha and dress-up use in-game points only (HP and CUS). They can't be bought with money.

VERSUS

Customer vs clerk, head to head.

Two players online. A match is two rounds, and you swap roles in between. The customer pushes ANGER up with complaints; the clerk wins them over with words.

Clerk
Win by calming them

Get CALM to 100 before ANGER hits 100.

VS

Customer
Win by making them snap

Push ANGER to 100 with pressure that fits your character. Over-the-line abuse backfires.

Ranked match
Play someone with the same language and a similar rating. Wins and losses change your rating.

Text or voice
Type your lines, or hold "push to talk" and argue out loud. Voice is transcribed and then scored.
