Contact

We accept inquiries about Kasuhara Defense Line by email (there is no contact form).

Email [Contact email]

Reporting a bug

It helps us a lot if you include:

  • What happened and the steps that led to it
  • The game version (bottom right of the title screen) and your Windows version
  • Your floor name (your name in versus), if you know it
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Deleting your account

You can delete it yourself in the game: Settings → Resign (delete data). If that doesn't work, for example because you can't connect to the server, email us with your floor name.

Abuse in versus matches

Please use the Report button during a match or on the match result screen. The match details reach us directly, so we can check it faster than by email.

About replies

Replies may take some time, and we may not be able to answer every message. We use what you send only to handle your inquiry (Privacy Policy).
