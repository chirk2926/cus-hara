When AI isn't available

The game keeps going even when the AI is having trouble. Solo play switches automatically to scripted customers and keyword scoring, so you can keep playing (scoring becomes a little simpler).

When you can't reach the server

Solo play with scripted customers works without the server. Versus, rankings and notices come back once the connection does. Please try again in a while.

If it still doesn't work

If it lasts a long time, please let us know via Contact.
