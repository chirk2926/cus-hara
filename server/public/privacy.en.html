3. How remarks in versus matches are handled (what you consent to)

Remarks in online versus matches are communications between users. The first time you play versus, the Game shows the details below and asks for your specific, explicit consent (ticking a box and pressing "Agree and continue to versus"). Without consent you cannot play versus, but solo mode remains available. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings (you won't be able to play versus after withdrawing).
  1. Checking and scoring: What you say in versus matches (text and voice) is checked for inappropriate content (sexual content, discrimination, threats, personal information) and scored by AI.
  2. Sending to external AI: For checking and scoring, the text of what you say (voice is transcribed first) is sent from the game server to an AI provider chosen by the operator (such as Anthropic). In voice matches, your voice is sent to a speech-to-text service.
  3. Delivery to your opponent: What you say is delivered to your opponent. Through Epic Online Services (Epic Games) voice chat, your voice is heard live while you talk (checks run after transcription, so the voice itself can't be stopped). When Epic isn't available, voice goes through the game server and only voice that passes the check is delivered.
  4. Storage: Remarks are kept in server memory only during the match and for 1 hour after it ends. Only for reported matches (reported by your opponent or by automatic detection) is the conversation text stored for 30 days, for the operator to review the report. Voice itself is never stored.
  5. Inappropriate remarks: Detected remarks are hidden from your opponent (as text) and the speaker receives a warning. Three warnings mean a loss, and the match is reported automatically.

If these terms change, we will ask for your consent again.

4. When the operator views conversations

The operator views stored conversations only to handle reports. Each viewing is recorded in the operator's admin log. The admin screen cannot be opened from outside the server.

5. Security

We encrypt traffic, store login tokens in a non-reversible form, keep secrets such as API keys separate, block outside access to the admin screen, and delete data automatically when its retention period ends.

6. Deletion and other requests

In Settings → "Resign (delete data)", you can delete the data on your PC and your game-server account (name, rating, titles, consent record and so on). However, to deal with abuse, reports made against you (1 year) and the conversations of reported matches (30 days) are kept until their retention period ends. Reports you made are kept with your identity removed. Match results are kept without names so opponents' ratings stay correct. If the server is unreachable, or for requests to disclose, correct or stop using your data, contact us at the address above.

7. Minors

If you are a minor, please obtain consent from a parent or guardian before using the Game.

8. Changes

We will announce changes to this policy on this page and through in-game news. Changes to section 3 require your consent again.
